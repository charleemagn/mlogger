# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MLogger to be a project where everyone feels welcome to ask a question, report a bug or propose a change — regardless of experience level, background or identity.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- being respectful and patient, especially with newcomers
- giving and accepting constructive feedback
- assuming good intent and focusing on the problem, not the person
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- insults, personal attacks and harassment of any kind
- sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- publishing someone else's private information without permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may block people who repeatedly ignore it.
